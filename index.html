About Henna

The Work works.
I know because it worked on me.

I'm a former corporate professional — fifteen years across accounting, strategy, and marketing. Thirteen years since then raising two kids and building a life from the inside out.

I've studied the Enneagram, Advaita Vedanta, Nonviolent Communication, and the Guru Granth Sahib. I've read the books, done the retreats, sat in the silence. What I know from all of it is this:

The fastest path out of suffering isn't insight. It's inquiry.

Byron Katie's The Work is the most precise, most reliable, most honest tool I've found for interrupting a stressful story and discovering what's actually true. I built this because I wanted that precision to be available to anyone — the moment they need it. Not after they've waited for an appointment, or calmed down enough to think clearly, or found the right book.

The moment you're triggered is the right moment. That's what this is built for.

The process

Five steps. One sitting.

Each step is a doorway — not a task. Move at your own pace.

1

Name what's weighing on you

List the people and situations you complain about — silently or aloud.

2

Get specific

Choose one moment. One person. One situation that's still with you.

3

Tell the story fully

The Judge-Your-Neighbor Worksheet — six statements, written without editing.

4

Question it

Four Questions and Turnarounds applied to each statement.

5

Come back to yourself

Discover what action is possible now that the story has loosened.
