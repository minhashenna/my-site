Logo 01 — When to use

  • Primary brand mark — nav, headers, email signatures
  • Horizontal layouts where width is available
  • Situations where the icon and name appear together
  • Social media profile images (icon mark only, cropped)

Logo 02 — When to use

  • Hero sections, print, and editorial contexts
  • Posters, presentations, and cover images
  • Square or vertical layouts
  • When a more editorial, typographic feel is needed
